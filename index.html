Our Story

Four generations beneath the same silver leaves.

Nestled in the hills between Aix and the sea, our grove has been tended by the same family since 1924. We grow the old varietals — Aglandau, Salonenque, Cayon — and press them the slow way, letting the fruit and the terroir speak for themselves.

Every bottle carries the taste of a single harvest: grassy, peppery, alive.
